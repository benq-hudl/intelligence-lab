StatsBomb Global Benchmark

All percentile scores are calculated against the StatsBomb Global Benchmark — players from the top 40 professional competitions worldwide, across the last 2 full seasons, filtered to the same role group.

This provides a large, stable benchmark population (~2,400 centre-backs, ~1,800 wingers, etc.) that does not fluctuate with season timing or competition selection.

The benchmark is independent of your viewing filter. Changing which competitions or seasons you view a player's data from does not change the population they are compared against.

Template creation — Position selection redesign
Comparing the current 24-position multi-select with the proposed simplified role group picker. The role group sets the benchmark pool — it answers "compared against whom?"
✕
⚠️ Current approach — 24 positions
User multi-selects from all 24 granular positions. The system then picks the single position with the most global minutes as the benchmark — invisibly.
🚨 With RCB, CB and LCB selected, the system silently benchmarks against LCB because it has the most minutes globally — the user never sees this.
✓ Proposed — Role group picker
User picks one role group. This directly sets the benchmark pool — transparent and simple. The metrics in the template define the style profile.
✅ Selecting "Centre Back" benchmarks against all CB / LCB / RCB players combined. What differentiates 8 striker templates is entirely the metrics they contain.
Key design decision
Template position selection serves one purpose only: defining the benchmark pool. The style of player is defined entirely by the metrics in the template. Separating these two concerns makes both simpler and more understandable.

Benchmark Position & Minutes

Benchmark Position sets two things: (1) the population your percentiles are calculated against, and (2) which profile templates are available. It defaults to the player's primary role group — the one where they've played the most minutes.

Minutes Included controls which of the player's own minutes feed into their p90 stats on the radar. By default, all minutes within the selected role group are included. You can refine this — for example, include only LW minutes when evaluating as a Winger, or add CAM minutes from another role group to see how output changes.

The benchmark population never changes when you adjust minutes — only the player's own data feed changes.
